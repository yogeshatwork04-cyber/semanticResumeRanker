Job Title: Digital Marketing Manager

About the Role
We are a growing e-commerce brand looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to
lead our online acquisition and retention programmes across search, social
and email channels.

Responsibilities
- Plan and run paid search and social campaigns on platforms such as Google Ads
  and Meta Ads, managing a monthly media budget.
- Improve organic traffic through SEO, keyword research and content marketing.
- Build and send email marketing programmes and lifecycle flows to grow repeat purchases.
- Track performance in Google Analytics and report results to leadership each month.
- Run A/B tests on landing pages, ad creatives and email subject lines.
- Work with the design and sales teams to launch seasonal campaigns on time.

Required Skills
- SEO
- Google Ads
- Google Analytics
- Content Marketing
- Email Marketing
- Campaign Management
- A/B Testing
- Budget Management

Preferred Skills
- HubSpot
- Meta Ads
- Marketing Automation
- SQL
- Looker Studio
- CRM

Qualifications
- Minimum 4 years of experience in digital marketing.
- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business or a related field. MBA is a plus.
